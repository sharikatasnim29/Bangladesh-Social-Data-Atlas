# Data note

Primary source: Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS) and UNICEF Bangladesh,
*Small Area Estimation: Upazila Level Prevalence of Participation in Organized Learning
and Child Marriage in Bangladesh*, April 2024.

The report states that MICS 2019 provides estimates at national, divisional and district
levels and that the small-area estimates combine MICS 2019 with Census 2011 auxiliary
information. This CSV keeps both the direct MICS district estimates and the SAE estimates.

For the first version of the atlas, use the **direct estimates** because they are the
survey estimates and are easiest to explain.
